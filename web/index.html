Don’t read the report. Run the model.

Paste an article. You get a short 3D film that shows how the topic works, then hands you the model to run with your own numbers, then lays out the options and leaves the choice to you.

How it compares with reading the report

  1. Explain

    A directed film follows the sunlight, power and money through a small world, one idea per shot.

  2. Simulate

    The page asks for your bill, your state and your habits, then runs the same world with your numbers.

  3. Decide

    Your options side by side, with the trade-offs, what the model leaves out, and questions to ask. It never tells you what to choose.
